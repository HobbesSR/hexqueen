How to play

  1. Place one queen bee in every coloured region.
  2. Every row and every diagonal in both directions holds exactly one queen.
  3. So no two queens share a region or a line, and queens never touch.

Controls

  • Tap once for ×, twice for a queen bee, three times to clear.
  • Drag across cells to mark many × at once.
  • Right-click toggles × with a mouse.
  • Arrows move, Space cycles, X, Q, H hint, Ctrl Z undo.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, and you can always reach it by deduction. No guessing needed.

Puzzle codes

The same code always builds the same board, so a friend can play the puzzle you just played.
